# ADI reasoning cycle

Evidence is graded fact / assumption / hearsay throughout. Options are compared against one shared set of criteria, never a different set each.

Task: $ARGUMENTS

## 1. Abduction — hypotheses

Generate two or three genuinely different candidate answers. Do not stop at the first one, and do not produce variations of a single idea dressed as alternatives.

## 2. Deduction — logic

For each hypothesis: is it internally consistent, does it fit the stated constraints, what breaks if it is wrong.

## 3. Induction — evidence

Look for support and refutation in the actual material: the repository, the code, the documents, the sources you can reach. Tag every piece of evidence as fact, assumption, or hearsay. An untagged claim is an assumption.

## 4. Audit — bias check

Am I anchored on the first hypothesis? Am I discounting evidence that cuts against it? Are the hypotheses actually diverse, or three shades of one? Did the scope come from analysis, or was it fitted to a conclusion I already had?

## 5. Decide

Pick the best hypothesis, comparing on the one shared set of criteria. State the rationale, then the residual uncertainty and what would change the answer.

**Output:** the structured analysis followed by a single recommendation. If the decision is architectural, the profile of characteristics is a separate step, not this one.
