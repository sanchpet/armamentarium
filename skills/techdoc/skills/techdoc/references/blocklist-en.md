# Final-pass blocklist: English

Applied ONLY in pass 3. Adapted from the kit shared by @lexfrei (sources: Wikipedia WP:AISIGNS, blader/humanizer catalog, Anbeeld/WRITING.md, community-confirmed tells 2026). Remove or replace every hit; the replacement comes from plain vocabulary, not a synonym of the banned word.

## Punctuation and formatting

- Em/en dash clusters. Two or more in a page of prose is the tell; one is fine.
- Curly quotes `“ ” ‘ ’` → straight quotes.
- Emoji, including 🚀 ✨ 🎉 ✅. Emoticons like `:P` are his and stay.
- Bold-term-colon list items (`- **Term**: explanation`, repeated) → prose or a plain list.
- Title Case Headings → sentence case.
- Bold for emphasis mid-sentence.

## Banned words (in prose; fine inside code, quotes, identifiers)

delve, tapestry, seamless, pivotal, crucial, vibrant, robustly, holistic, multifaceted, paradigm, leverage (as verb), harness (as verb), foster, empower, unlock, unveil, elevate, streamline, underscore (as verb), testament, landscape (figurative), realm, boast, showcase (as verb), journey (figurative), comprehensive (as filler), invaluable, game-changer, cutting-edge, ever-evolving.

## Banned phrases

- "It's worth noting", "It is important to note", "Please note that"
- "I hope this helps", "Let me know if", "Feel free to"
- "Great question", "Great point", "You're absolutely right"
- "In summary", "In conclusion", "Overall,", "To summarize"
- "Let's dive in", "deep dive into", "Here's the thing", "Here's a breakdown"
- "not just X, (but) Y", "not only … but also"
- "stands as", "serves as a", "plays a (key/crucial) role", "a testament to"
- "in today's world", "in the ever-evolving landscape"
- "Additionally," / "Moreover," / "Furthermore," as paragraph openers
- "significant(ly)" as a vague intensifier without a number from the source material
- Rhetorical question openers: "What makes this work?", "Why does this matter?"
- "Read that again", "Let that sink in", "The truth is"

## Structural (pass 2 should have caught these)

- Three-item lists where two items carry the content.
- Every paragraph the same length.
- A final paragraph restating the body.
